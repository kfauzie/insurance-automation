# Glossary: Fernway

> Rows marked "proposed" are standard industry terms not yet confirmed as Fernway's own words.

| Term | Plain meaning | Unit | Don't confuse with |
|---|---|---|---|
| Claim | A policyholder's request to be paid under their policy | | A complaint |
| Claims handler | The Fernway person who works a claim and makes the final call | | The AI, which suggests |
| TPA | Third-party administrator: a firm that handles claims on an insurer's behalf | | The insurer |
| MGA | Managing general agent: writes policies for an insurer; a Fernway client | | Broker |
| FNOL (proposed) | First notice of loss: when the claim is first reported | | Claim decision |
| Leakage (proposed) | Money paid out that shouldn't have been | $ or % of paid | Fraud (one cause of leakage) |
| Cost per claim | What it costs Fernway to handle one claim | $ per claim | Claim payout |
| Time to settle | From claim reported to paid or closed | days | Handler time per claim |
